# Koko Eating Bananas

**Difficulty:** Medium

Koko has piles of bananas and h hours before the guards return. Each hour she chooses one pile and eats up to k bananas from it; if the pile has fewer than k, she finishes that pile and waits for the next hour. Find the minimum integer eating speed k so that she can finish all the piles within h hours.

## Example 1

Input: piles = [3, 6, 7, 11], h = 8
Output: 4

## Example 2

Input: piles = [30, 11, 23, 4, 20], h = 5
Output: 30

Constraints: there is at least one pile, and h is at least as large as the number of piles.
